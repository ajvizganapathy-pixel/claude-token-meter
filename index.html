Before You Flash
  • Connect your XIAO ESP32-S3 via USB-C to your computer.
  • Enter bootloader mode: hold the BOOT button (B), press and release RESET (R), then release BOOT. The board will appear as a serial port — no LED blink is normal.
  • Click Download Latest Firmware below to fetch the binary automatically, or select a local file.
  • Click Connect, pick the serial port, then Flash. The board resets automatically when done.

After Flashing
  • The device reboots and creates an open WiFi AP: ClaudeTokenMeter
  • Connect your phone/laptop to ClaudeTokenMeter (no password). A captive portal opens — if not, go to 192.168.4.1.
  • Enter your home WiFi credentials, optional Anthropic API key, and weekly token limit. Click Save.
  • Run the companion script: python companion/claude_monitor.py --discover
